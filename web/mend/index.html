Mend

How to play

Every line of tiles is a real word with its letters mixed up: put them back before your swaps run out.

The words are TOE and GOAT. Swap the top A with the yellow T in the row and both go green. Now the E and the last T are grey: each belongs in the other’s word, so one more swap finishes it.
1

Tap two tiles, or drag one onto the other, to swap them. A swap can’t be undone. Par is the fewest it can be done in: you get par + 3 swaps, and what’s left is your score.

2

Green is in place and stays put. Yellow is wanted elsewhere in a word through that tile, either word where two cross. Grey isn’t wanted in its own words, but may belong in another.

3

A word only lights as many of a letter as it still needs: if it shows two Es and wants one more, one is yellow and one grey.

The week gets harder: bigger boards, fewer tiles in place and trickier openings, some showing a real word in the wrong place.
